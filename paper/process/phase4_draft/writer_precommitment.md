# Phase 4a writer pre-commitment

Contract: `academic_paper/writer_full/v1`

Paper metadata: *CalibAgent: Task-Aware Active Calibration and Shift Recovery
for Quadruped Velocity Commands*; robotics/engineering; target body length
3,700 words.

## Acceptance Criteria Paraphrase

### D1: section_completeness

The draft will contain every approved section and will not pass forward with
unresolved material placeholders.

### D2: citation_density

External factual claims will be tied to verified bibliography entries. Claims
from the authors' experiments will be tied to named versioned evidence rather
than presented as unsupported general facts.

### D3: argument_blueprint_fidelity

Each section will implement the approved claim-evidence-reasoning chain,
including the specified counterarguments and evidence boundaries.

### D4: total_word_count

The complete body will remain within ten percent of the declared target unless
the eight-page compiled limit requires a documented shorter form.

### D5: per_section_word_count

Each section will stay within fifteen percent of its approved allocation, with
any necessary compression reported rather than hidden.

### D6: paragraph_structure

At least eighty percent of substantive paragraphs will open with a claim,
present evidence, interpret it, and connect it to the section purpose.

### D7: register_consistency

The manuscript will maintain precise engineering prose, stable terminology,
and consistent IEEE-style scholarly register throughout.
