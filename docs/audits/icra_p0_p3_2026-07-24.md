## Claim boundary and limitations

The verdict supports real passive P1 calibration plus the frozen synthetic P2
and P3 claims. It does not support real-robot online P3 execution or later
P4–P8 claims.

The real dataset used native approximately 20 Hz LiDAR odometry instead of the
50 Hz acquisition target, has no full rosbag/video, contains incomplete device
metadata, and covers one robot/environment/date. These facts are versioned in
the manifest and report; the rate deviation has a mandatory sensitivity gate.
